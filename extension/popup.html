How it works
  1. Save a budget. This is the most capital the bot may risk, including open positions, reserved orders, and realized losses.
  2. Choose an approach, then Start. Jev can buy, hold, or sell and choose small, medium, or maximum affordable size. Long-shot rests bids on favourites and normally holds them to the result.
  3. Watch its reason. Waiting is a decision. Stale data, no liquidity, insufficient budget, or an unresolved order prevents a new order.
Orders run on the server across supported BTC markets. You do not need extra Kalshi tabs. Manual Kalshi trades are separate and can pause reconciliation.
A high win rate can still lose money: long-shot bids risk much more than they earn on each win. Paper results remain available on the dashboard.

  
Connection & recovery
Market scanning continues while orders are blocked. Previously running strategies recover after three clean account checks following a restart or temporary connection problem. Manual Stop stays stopped.
